Our Services

We cater to all the needs you have about your dental health. We employ only the best and use the highest quality equipment to provide the best service.

Dental Check-Up

This is a periodic dental check, where dentist examines the teeth for any abnormality. Dental check-up is a preventive step towards maintaining a healthy oral hygiene.

Scale & Polish

This is the procedure of professionally cleaning the teeth and gums. It involves removing staining, plaque (soft, sticky bacteria) and tartar build up.

Teeth Whitening

This is a procedure that involves the lightening of the exterior surface of the teeth. It includes sanding down stains, bleaching, UV light therapy, and more.

Endodontics

This focuses on caring for complex tooth problems that primarily affect the tooth pulp. It involves advanced techniques to treat the dental pulp and root tissues.

Periodontal Treatment

This focuses exclusively in the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of inflammatory diseases of the tissues and structures surrounding the teeth.

Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontics is a specialised domain of dentistry involved in the correction of teeth misalignment, protrusion and crowded teeth.

Paediatric Dentistry

Dental Signature team expend more time, and extra effort, in ensuring that our little patients are comfortable and happy during their visit.

Dental Cleaning

The Dental Signature team ensure we deliver a thorough dental cleaning job in order to improve the oral hygiene of our esteemed patients.

Extraction Procedure

Tooth extractions are sometimes carried out to reduce crowding and to eliminate badly damaged teeth or fractured teeth.

Dental Filling

This involves the stuffing of the hard surfaces of the teeth that are lost through decay caused by bacteria, broken, worn-out or chipped portions.
